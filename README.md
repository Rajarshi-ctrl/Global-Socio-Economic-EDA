# Global Socio-Economic Indicators Analysis (1970–2020)

Comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of global socio-economic and education indicators spanning 195+ countries over 50 years using World Bank EdStats data.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Global socio-economic metrics across literacy rates, education expenditure, and demographic shifts are multi-dimensional and complex. Policy research and data analytics require structured data cleaning, longitudinal trend modeling, and comparative regional benchmarks to derive actionable insights.

## 💡 Solution Architecture
- **Longitudinal EDA & Cleaning:** Cleaned and imputed missing values across 50 years of World Bank socio-economic time-series datasets.
- **Multivariate Data Analysis:** Evaluated correlations between government education spending, secondary/tertiary literacy rates, and regional GDP indicators.
- **Interactive Visualizations:** Built high-impact interactive dashboards and distribution charts using Plotly, Seaborn, and Matplotlib.

## 🚀 Business & Technical Impact
- **Data Analyst:** Delivered clear comparative trend insights across 195+ countries and regional groupings.
- **Data Scientist:** Formulated statistical correlation matrices and country clustering to identify socio-economic progress tiers.
- **Data Engineer:** Structured clean tabular data pipelines to standardize raw World Bank EdStats CSV inputs into analysis-ready formats.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
- **Languages:** Python 3.8+
- **Data Manipulation:** Pandas, NumPy
- **Visualization:** Plotly, Seaborn, Matplotlib
- **Dataset:** World Bank EdStats Database (1970–2020)
